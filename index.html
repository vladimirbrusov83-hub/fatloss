Fat loss, checked against the research

Lose fat, keep your muscle, skip the myths

The questions my clients actually ask: how much to eat, why the scale stalls, whether diet breaks work, how to keep the weight off. Each gets a straight answer, the study behind it, and what to do about it.

Every page tells you who was studied. Most nutrition advice quotes research done on untrained, older, or clinically obese people and passes it off as a finding about lifters. Where that gap exists, it's stated on the page.
Overstated

Can a calorie calculator tell me exactly how much to eat?

The best equation lands within about 10% for most people. Start there, then let two weeks of your own data set the number.

Frankenfield 2005 · systematic review Mostly true

How fast can I lose weight without losing muscle?

Roughly 0.5–1% of bodyweight per week. Faster costs you lean mass and strength.

Garthe 2011 · Iraki 2019 True

Do I have to be in a calorie deficit to lose fat?

Yes. Locked-ward studies where every calorie was measured settle this one.

Hall 2015 · metabolic ward feeding trial False

Is there one best diet for fat loss?

Low-carb, low-fat and named diets tie head to head. Sticking with it predicted results; the diet type didn't.

Johnston 2014 · 48 RCTs · 7,286 people Overstated

Do you just have to put up with hunger when dieting?

Protein, high-volume foods, fewer ultra-processed foods and enough sleep all reduce it.

Kohanmoo 2020 · Hall 2019 · Nedeltcheva 2010 Mostly true

How much protein do I need to build muscle?

About 1.6 g/kg/day is where the added benefit runs out in the pooled data. Higher isn't harmful; it's just not doing more.

Morton 2018 · 49 RCTs · 1,863 people Overstated

My weight hasn't moved in two weeks. Has my diet stopped working?

Water swings hide fat loss over a week or two. Judge the weekly average over three weeks before you change anything.

Kanellakis 2023 · Madigan 2015 Overstated

Does "starvation mode" stop you losing weight?

Metabolic adaptation is real but small. A stalled scale is almost always creeping calories.

Nunes 2022 · systematic review Depends

Do diet breaks help you lose more fat?

In lifters: the same fat loss with less hunger. In men with obesity: more fat lost per week of dieting. Both take longer.

Byrne 2018 · Peos 2021 ICECAP Depends

Does your body have a set point it fights to defend?

Your body pushes back when you lose weight, but within a range that your habits and environment can move.

Speakman 2011 · Müller 2013 Overstated

Does everyone regain the weight after a diet?

Most people regain a lot, but not all of it. The ones who keep it off share a few habits.

Anderson 2001 · 29 studies · NWCR Overstated

Can you burn fat off one specific spot?

Most trials say no, a couple find a small effect. Some areas really are slower; total fat loss fixes them.

Vispute 2011 · Brobakken 2023 Overstated

Is fasted cardio better for fat loss?

Fasting shifts which fuel you burn. Eating first improves long sessions. Neither was shown to make you leaner.

Aird 2018 · systematic review Depends

Can I build muscle and lose fat at the same time?

Yes for beginners, returnees, and people carrying more fat. Marginal if you're lean and trained.

Longland 2016 RCT · no meta-analysis exists Depends

Does intermittent fasting beat normal eating for body composition?

It beat non-fasting controls on fat loss, but those controls weren't calorie-matched. A schedule, not a mechanism.

Keenan 2020 · Ashtary-Larky 2021 False

Do I need 6 small meals a day to stoke my metabolism?

No. The pooled effect vanished once one outlier study was removed.

Schoenfeld 2015 · 15 studies Overstated

Does eating late at night make you fat?

Late eating does change hunger and energy expenditure, but calories still decide the outcome.

Vujović 2022 · Cell Metab Depends

Will drinking alcohol ruin my gains?

Dose matters enormously, and the honest answer is that the human evidence here is thin.

Parr 2014 · limited evidence Overstated

Is there a 30 g protein-per-meal cap?

The cap is soft, not a wall. Aim ~0.4 g/kg per meal and stop worrying about the ceiling.

Schoenfeld 2018 · review False

Does high protein damage your kidneys?

Not in people with healthy kidneys. 28 trials found no difference in kidney function.

Devries 2018 · 28 trials · 1,358 people Mostly true

Plant vs animal protein: can vegans build as much muscle?

Strength is a wash. A small animal edge for muscle mass, but soy matched milk head to head.

Lim 2021 · Reid-McCann 2025 Overstated

Is there an anabolic window after training?

The window is hours wide, not 30 minutes. Total daily protein is what moved the needle once intake was equated.

Schoenfeld 2013 · 23 trials Mostly true

Clean bulk vs dirty bulk: how big should my surplus be?

Muscle gain has a speed limit. Past a modest surplus, the extra is fat.

Slater 2019 · Iraki 2019
